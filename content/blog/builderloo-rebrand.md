I created a new website. Well, actually I rebranded an old one that I wasn't really happy with. The new site is called [builderloo.ca](https://builderloo.ca).

## Why it exists

I created this website because I really wanted visibility into the Waterloo tech ecosystem. There's a lot of startups, there's a lot of events, there's a lot of people building really cool stuff, but there isn't a place to actually go and see it.

So I made that website, and originally it took the shape of kwbuilds.ca. But the site itself wasn't really engaging. It was very black and white. So with Builderloo, I wanted to go in a different direction. Builderloo has a bright, colourful theme that in my mind represents what Waterloo Region tech is.

kwbuilds.ca still works. Every old link sends you to the same page on builderloo.ca.

## What's on it

On the website you'll find a list of startups that are local to the Waterloo Region, and the events taking place throughout the region. There are also news articles that mention startups operating here, and a jobs page.

On September 30 the site listed 698 local startups, 79 upcoming events and 114 open roles in the region.

## The forum

All of that's great, but I wasn't really happy with just leaving it there. The American tech sector has their own platform. They have Hacker News. And it's very saturated in American content. There isn't really a place built for Canadian entrepreneurs and Canadian builders.

So I wanted to create a local forum where people could post about the stuff they're building, whether that's a startup or something as simple as a GitHub project. It's super simple. You post a link or a description of whatever you're working on, and you can get points and get visibility for your project.

Start a title with Show:, Ask:, Jobs: or Event: and it goes in that section.

## The Weekly

I've also restarted the KWBuilds newsletter under Builderloo. Every Monday morning you'll get a report on the week before: the new startups that joined, the startups mentioned in the news, who's hiring, and a bunch of other numbers about the ecosystem we live in.

You can sign up on [builderloo.ca](https://builderloo.ca).

If you're building something in Waterloo Region, post it on the forum.

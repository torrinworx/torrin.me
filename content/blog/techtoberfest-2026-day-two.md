## A showcase instead of a pitch competition

Sheldon said this was an experiment. Techtoberfest used to have a pitch competition. This year the founders on stage were not all asking for money. Some already have it, and some have customers paying the bills. The point was to show what is being built here.

![The founder showcase, sponsored by PwC](showcase-title.jpg)

Devin was the MC. He split the eight founders into three groups: atoms, qubits and bits.

## Atoms {animate: atoms}

### Finite Robotics

Matt Stevens of [Finite Robotics](https://builderloo.ca/s/finiterobotics.com) opened with apples. Machines now pick most of the crops we know how to grow without people, and we grow far more of those than of the crops that still need hands, like fruit. A hundred million dollars has gone into apple-picking robots without a working one. Finite works on thinning instead: counting the tiny green fruit early in the season and cutting off the extra. He said people are bad at counting it. He has counted the same tree twice and got two different answers.

### Poppy

Jay from [Poppy](https://builderloo.ca/s/talkpoppy.com) showed a walkie-talkie for kids four and up. It has no screen, no ads and no subscription. A kid holds a button and talks to grandma. An optional add-on answers questions, and he played a clip of his son asking it where mangoes grow. Pre-orders open in the next month or two at [talkpoppy.com](https://talkpoppy.com).

Sam, the CTO of [Upside Robotics](https://builderloo.ca/s/upsiderobotics.com), said most fertilizer farmers put on a field never reaches the crop. Their small solar robots drive between the rows and put down a little every week instead of a lot at once.

### CSMC

Adam Gray, the COO of [the Canadian Strategic Missions Corporation](https://builderloo.ca/s/strategicmissions.ca), talked about micro nuclear power. His case was that grids are running out of room, and not mostly because of AI. They have 31 government contracts worth $23 million so far. Why build in Waterloo? "Because in Waterloo we like doing hard" things. He used a stronger word.

## Qubits {animate: qubits}

Devin said quantum has always been "ten years away," and that this is changing now.

### Qubic

Margaret Panetta, the CTO of [Qubic](https://builderloo.ca/s/qubictech.co), showed the very small superconducting circuits they make in the University of Waterloo's clean room, for quantum computers and quantum sensors. They are a team of 10. Eight are PhD scientists and engineers, and five did their degrees at Waterloo and stayed.

### UpScale Quantum

Alex Cooper of [UpScale Quantum](https://builderloo.ca/s/upscalequantumsolutions.com) has spent seven years building his technology in a lab at the University of Waterloo. [Garage Capital](https://builderloo.ca/s/garage.vc) and Golden wrote the first cheque. He talked about defence uses and about GPS signals being jammed.

## Bits {animate: bits}

### AllMind

Anwaar Malik of [AllMind](https://builderloo.ca/s/allmind.ai) builds research tools for hedge funds and asset managers. His point was that checking one new idea against all of a fund's data takes a team about three weeks. AllMind connects over 7,000 data sets with a fund's own systems so the question can be asked in one place.

### Ground News

Harleen Kaur closed the showcase. [Ground News](https://builderloo.ca/s/ground.news) shows every story with the outlets covering it and how each one leans, so you can see what your side isn't reading. It was built in Kitchener-Waterloo over seven years. Readers pay for it, and she said that's why it answers to readers.

Day one was investors talking about money. Day two was founders talking about what they make. I learned quite a bit from both days.

I didn't know Waterloo had so many farm robots. [Finite](https://builderloo.ca/s/finiterobotics.com) thins apple trees and [Upside](https://builderloo.ca/s/upsiderobotics.com) fertilizes corn, and both build here. Most of what I'd heard about local tech before was software. Glad to learn more about this side of the tech community.

Nearly everyone I talked to asked the same thing in some form: where do I find out what's happening here? Funding, launches, who's hiring. That's why I started [Builderloo](https://builderloo.ca), and two days of people asking it made me want to keep going.

Next time I'll bring stickers.
